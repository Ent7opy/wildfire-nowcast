# Project Overview

Wildfire Nowcast exists to turn noisy wildfire-related signals into clear, time-bounded decisions.

## Core Product Shape

- **Observe** what is happening now.
- **Project** what is likely next.
- **Prioritize** where attention should go.
- **Communicate** outputs in a way that supports action.

## Design Constraints

- Global coverage with graceful regional specialization.
- Transparent assumptions and confidence.
- Repeatable pipelines and auditable outputs.
- Simple interfaces for non-ML users.
