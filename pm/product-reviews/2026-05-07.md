## 1. Thesis adherence

The built surface broadly matches the thesis. The landing page (`app/page.tsx`) carries the canonical positioning line verbatim, frames stewardship explicitly, and refuses the "fastest alert to largest audience" frame. The data path — FIRMS poll (`lib/firms/client.ts`) → spatial matcher (`lib/firms/matcher.ts`) → gate (`lib/ai/gate.ts`, four conditions matching SPEC §Flow 6 exactly) → structured brief (`lib/ai/generate.ts`, `lib/ai/schema.ts`) → email dispatch (`lib/notify/dispatch.ts`) → per-AOI history (`app/dashboard/aoi/[id]/page.tsx`) — is the pipeline the thesis describes. `lib/ai/prompt.ts` and the `prior_events` plumbing in `lib/ai/generate.ts:250` show the team understood that "site's own history" is the L2 differentiator and didn't quietly drop it.

The gap is in **what makes a brief feel like the worked example in SPEC §LLM brief format**. The schema fields are present (`weather_note`, `authority_perimeter`, `prior_events`), but `weather_note` and `authority_perimeter` are nullable-by-design in v1 (SPEC open question 3 punted authority perimeters to v1.1; weather is not visibly wired) and `prior_events` is sourced only from prior `aoi_events` rows in this same database — i.e. a brand-new AOI on a never-burned-before deployment will *always* render "no prior events on file." The thesis's load-bearing sample paragraph ("Fuel moisture in this stand is below the post-2023-Mati regrowth threshold. Two comparable spread events near Evia produced…") cannot be produced by this pipeline yet, because the inputs that would let an LLM say it aren't being fetched. The brief is currently "L1.5" — structured, AOI-scoped, schema-validated, but thin. Not a thesis violation; a thesis-adherence-pending-data-fanout flag.

## 2. Flow coherence

1. **Discover.** No marketing surface beyond `/`. Acceptance item 9 (LTA WRN newsletter post) is held until launch. There is no LTA-flavored copy, no example AOI, no screenshot, no second page. A first-time visitor sees one screen of prose, then "Sign in." Newsletter readers will need more.
2. **Sign-up.** Clerk wired (`app/sign-in`, `app/sign-up`, `app/webhooks/clerk`). Webhook secret still on `pm/blockers.md` as the only active blocker — JIT path papers over first-request, but email change / deletion will not propagate until Vanyo lands the secret. Functional but incomplete.
3. **Create AOI.** `/dashboard/aoi/new` accepts paste-GeoJSON and file upload. **Map drawing is not implemented** (`page.tsx:1` comment "No map drawing (v1.1)") and **bbox paste is not implemented**. SPEC US-1 acceptance is "at least two of the three work in v1" — the surface ships exactly two (paste, upload), both of which assume the user already has a `.geojson` file. A land trust steward who has their preserve in ArcGIS / Google Earth and not as a flat GeoJSON file is one Google away, but this is the friction point a 5-minute cold-start needs to nail. **Flow break risk: the 5-minute SLA in US-1 acceptance has not been measured and the polygon-input UX is the most likely place it fails.**
4. **AOI is monitored.** Cron is real (`app/api/aoi/poll/route.ts`, 468 LOC, GH Actions wired per PR #387). Iterates all users (Stage 5).
5. **Detection triggers brief.** Gate is implemented and tested. Brief generated via AI Gateway → Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, schema-validated.
6. **Brief is dispatched.** Resend email working (`lib/notify/dispatch.ts`, 527 LOC — large for what it does, worth a future reviewer pass). **Webhook channel is recorded in schema but explicitly not implemented** (`schema/index.ts:276`: `"webhook" reserved (always recorded as skipped)`). SPEC US-5 (snooze/pause/unsubscribe links) — `grep` finds **no snooze/unsubscribe link in `lib/notify/markdown.ts`**. The token-signed snooze flow that US-5 acceptance requires does not exist.
7. **User reads brief, makes a decision.** `/dashboard/brief/[id]` renders Markdown-as-HTML (via `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` — note: `lib/notify/markdown.ts` better be a sanitizing renderer, worth a security-review). Share-token flow shipped (`app/api/brief/[id]/share`, `app/brief/share/[token]`).
8. **Refine rules.** `RulesForm` shipped. SPEC US-2 acceptance includes "quiet-hours detections are held and delivered in a morning digest" — looking at the dispatcher, quiet-hours appears to **suppress, not defer**. Worth verifying.
9. **AOI detail page.** Shipped, but the `aoi/[id]` page renders `BBox: [...] · TODO v1.1: MapLibre` as literal user-facing text. **No map at all.** SPEC US-4 acceptance: "polygon on MapLibre, last 90 days of detections as points" — neither half exists. The whole point of "watching a polygon" is the polygon being visible.

**Net:** the *backend* pipeline is end-to-end. The *user-facing* surface has two real holes (no map anywhere; no snooze/unsubscribe in emails) and one tooling hole (no draw / bbox-paste, only file upload). A non-Vanyo first user who has a `.geojson` file and wants only email can complete the loop today. A non-Vanyo first user from LTA WRN who shows up with a Map-Maker-drawn polygon they have not yet exported, or who wants to verify what's being watched, cannot.

## 3. Feature audit

**Built but unnecessary:**

- `lib/ai/generate.ts` is 519 LOC — likely justified by the structured-output ceremony, but worth a `simplify` pass.
- `lib/notify/dispatch.ts` at 527 LOC for "send Resend email + record row" suggests scope creep into rate-limiting, quiet-hours, dedupe, and error policy that may exceed v1 needs. Not unnecessary, possibly over-developed for the user count (zero) it serves.
- `share` system (`lib/share/token.ts`, `share-toggle`, public share page) is fully built. SPEC US-6 calls for grant-report exports; the public 30-day share link is from US-4. Useful, but it's polish that came before the 5-minute cold-start was measured. No concerns about the feature itself; it landed before more load-bearing UX did.
- `cost_usd_est` and `latency_ms` columns on `aoi_briefs` and surfaced on the brief footer (`brief/[id]/page.tsx:46`). This is the kind of operator-telemetry-leaking-into-user-UI that the IDC framing produced and the stewardship thesis explicitly rejected. A land trust steward does not want to see *"cost-est: $–"* under their fire brief. **Cut from the user-facing footer; keep in the DB for ops.**

**Missing but needed (thesis-required, not in code or backlog):**

- **A polygon picker.** Only paste/upload. The SPEC's own "at least two of three" gives the team an out, but the thesis's 5-minute cold-start almost certainly demands "draw on map" because that's how a land trust GIS-novice operates. Backlog should reflect this as launch-blocking, not v1.1.
- **A map.** No MapLibre, no Deck.GL anywhere in `app/` or `lib/` (verified by grep). SPEC US-4 explicitly requires a map on the AOI page. Backlog should track this as launch-blocking; right now Stage 5/6 marked "merged" implies completeness.
- **Snooze / pause / unsubscribe email links** (US-5). The schema has `pausedUntil`, the dispatcher honors it, but there's no signed-token endpoint a user can hit from an email. Without this, "control without logging in" — a real user expectation for email alerts — is missing.
- **Onboarding / "first poll scheduled" confirmation email** (SPEC Flow 1 step 5). Not visible in code. The 5-minute acceptance criterion is measured against this email; the system can't pass acceptance without it.
- **Some way to validate "did the brief help me."** No feedback link, no "was this useful," no opt-in survey. For a tool whose core thesis claim is *L2 depth over L1 speed*, having zero feedback channel means "is this differentiated" cannot be answered post-launch. Not in backlog.
- **Authority perimeter fetch** (SPEC open question 3). Schema field exists, always null. Briefs without authority context are noticeably thinner than the thesis sample paragraph. SPEC marks this as v1.1; given thesis adherence, it should be re-prioritized ahead of Stage 6's nice-to-haves.
- **Backfill on first AOI** (Flow 1 step 6: "First cron tick within 15 min runs a backfill poll over last 24 h"). Not visible. A user arriving mid-fire gets nothing for up to 15 minutes.

**Built but underdeveloped:**

- **The brief itself.** `prior_events` only sources from this DB's `aoi_events` history. For new AOIs (i.e., every AOI on launch day), prior_events is empty. The thesis brief leans heavily on *"comparable past events at this site."* Without a historical-fire dataset (MTBS, EFFIS perimeters, GlobFire), the brief is generic. This is the load-bearing thesis claim and the gap is invisible from the merged PR list.
- **Landing page.** Single page, single CTA. No "what does a brief look like" sample. Newsletter-driven cold visitors will bounce.
- **AOI detail page.** No map, no detections plotted, no bbox visualization beyond two text-printed corner coordinates. Functionally a list of links.

## 4. Strategic blind spots

**Distribution.** Acceptance item 9 (LTA WRN newsletter draft) is the entire GTM and it's gated on items 1–8 passing. There is **no second distribution channel staged** — no IPBN contact, no EUROPARC outreach, no Firewise contact, no LTER contact. If the LTA newsletter slot doesn't materialize or doesn't convert, there's no plan B. Eight archetypes were named in the thesis; v1 commits to one. That's correct narrowing for the build, but the team has not staged outreach to the *next* archetype, which means a 4–8 week silent gap if archetype 1 doesn't bite.

**No feedback loop with non-Vanyo humans.** SPEC open question 6 ("ship the spec and let the newsletter post be the interview") was never answered. Zero user interviews have been conducted (per PM_CLAUDE constraint), and the 5-minute cold-start, the brief's perceived usefulness, and the email's quiet-hours behavior have never been observed by anyone outside the agent loop. The maturity gate (PM_CLAUDE §Maturity gate) explicitly required "evidence from ≥2 independent sources" for a candidate problem; that was satisfied for *direction*, but the *artifact* has not been put in front of any of the named archetypes. Loop trust has been increased (ADR 0007) without a corresponding user-feedback mechanism — the feedback loop is reviewer-LGTM, not user-finds-this-useful.

**Competitor risk.** Watch Duty has signaled US-only consumer alerts; the thesis assumes WD doesn't reach stewardship. WD's 2025–2026 partnership directions aren't tracked anywhere in `pm/`. If WD adds a "watch a polygon" feature for non-profits — a one-week move — most of the thesis's positioning evaporates. There is no `pm/signals/` entry tracking WD product news since the pivot research.

**Vercel Hobby non-commercial clause** (R1 in pivot-architecture). Pre-launch confirmation was a mitigation. There is no evidence in `pm/blockers.md` or anywhere that Vercel was actually contacted. This is the biggest infra risk to launch and it's silently unresolved.

**Neon free tier ceiling.** R5 says 14-day TTL prune. Looking at `firms_detections` schema, I see no prune logic referenced from the cron route. Worth verifying — at 100 AOIs across the world, FIRMS detection rows accumulate fast in fire season.

**FIRMS rate limits.** R3 mitigation is "deterministic 5°×5° bucket hashing already coalesces; add jitter + exponential backoff; circuit-breaker." The cron route is 468 LOC; without re-reading it carefully, I can't assert whether FIRMS-429 actually circuit-breaks gracefully or whether the system silently drops a polling tick during a major event — *the moment the system most needs to be honest about its limits*. SPEC has no acceptance criterion that the system tells the *user* "we missed a tick" when this happens. **The system is not honest about its limits to its user.** That is a thesis violation: a stewardship tool's core promise is "we are watching"; if we silently aren't, the trust contract is broken.

**ADR 0007 (loosened auto-merge).** Removed Vanyo from the gate for everything except harness-self-modifying files. The mitigation is "the reviewer agent becomes the only adversarial check." That is a single point of failure with no monitoring beyond Vanyo manually reading PRs. Combined with the lack of any user feedback channel, the loop now has no external validation at all. I think the trade-off is right (Vanyo bottleneck was real), but it's worth tracking how many merges have post-merge reverts as a proxy for reviewer rigor.

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Add (launch-blocking):** Map on `/dashboard/aoi/[id]` and `/dashboard/aoi/new`. MapLibre + a Polygon draw control. SPEC US-4 acceptance can't pass without it, and "watching a polygon" without seeing the polygon is not the product. This was deferred to v1.1 in the AOI new page; that deferral was wrong.

2. **Add (launch-blocking):** Snooze / pause / unsubscribe signed-token endpoints + email links. US-5 acceptance is concrete; stewardship users on alert systems expect them; current state will generate "how do I stop this" tickets immediately. ~1 day work given share-token plumbing already exists.

3. **Investigate:** measure the cold-start path against US-1's 5-minute SLA on a clean browser with a real LTA-style polygon (something traceable like a Nature Conservancy preserve from public GIS). If it fails, that's the priority over polishing Stage 6 features.

4. **Cut (from user UI, not DB):** model name, prompt version, gate reason, latency ms, cost-est USD from the brief footer (`app/dashboard/brief/[id]/page.tsx:43–47`). Stewardship users do not want to see operator telemetry under their fire brief; this is residual IDC framing. Keep the columns and surface them on a `/admin` page or in `job_runs`.

5. **Add:** authority-perimeter fetch as an LLM tool-call (per SPEC open question 3's preferred answer). One LLM tool call into NIFC / ICNF / CWFIS public GeoJSON per brief. Without it, the briefs feel templatey — they don't say "the authority perimeter posted 90 minutes ago covers the detection cluster" because that data isn't fetched. This is the single highest-leverage move for thesis adherence.

6. **Add:** a one-line "Did this brief help?" yes/no link in every email + a `brief_feedback` column. One signal beats zero. Shipping anything without a feedback channel is shipping blind.

7. **Investigate:** does the cron honestly degrade on FIRMS 429 / partial returns? Add a `data_freshness` field to the AOI page so the user knows "last polled 47 min ago, 1 retry pending, no detections" instead of seeing no briefs and assuming all is calm.

8. **Add:** prune logic for `firms_detections` older than 14 days, invoked from the cron. Verify if it exists; I didn't find it in the file list. R5 mitigation depends on it.

9. **Investigate:** Vercel Hobby non-commercial clause. Send the pre-launch confirmation email Vanyo flagged in R1. Until this is in writing from Vercel, the launch infra plan has an unresolved legal risk.

10. **Reframe:** the "Stages 3–6 merged" framing in `CLAUDE.md` overstates v1 readiness. Stages-merged ≠ acceptance-criteria-passing. Add a separate `pm/launch-readiness.md` mapping each `docs/SPEC-A-prime-v1.md` acceptance item (1–10) to evidence. Right now the only person who knows what's actually shippable is Vanyo, mentally.

11. **Add:** a second-archetype outreach plan in `pm/`. One contact each for IPBN, EUROPARC, IJNR. Don't email yet; have the contact identified. If LTA WRN doesn't bite, week 2 of post-launch shouldn't be a blank week.
