# Wildfire Nowcast 101

## Purpose

Build a trustworthy wildfire intelligence product that helps people make better decisions over the next 24-72 hours.

## Primary Users

- Incident and operations analysts
- Public-interest researchers and NGOs
- Media and monitoring teams

## Core Outcomes

- Current wildfire activity is easy to inspect.
- Near-term spread is understandable and comparable across areas.
- Risk and uncertainty are visible enough to support prioritization.

## Product Boundaries

In scope:

- Nowcast, short-horizon forecast, and risk interpretation
- Area-based exploration and summary outputs
- Exportable, reproducible decision artifacts

Out of scope:

- Fully automated operational decision making
- Long-range climate forecasting
- Replacing official emergency command systems

## Quality Bar

- Outputs are timely, explainable, and versioned.
- Confidence and limitations are always communicated.
- Changes are evaluated against real operational usefulness.
